    About Paperscope

    Paperscope searches free scholarly databases, merges the results and ranks them against your query and, if you add one, your research profile. It is not Google Scholar and does not search the same sources or reproduce its ranking. It is free to use and needs no paid AI service.

    What leaves your device

    InformationSent to
    Your CV or statement, and the profile made from itNowhere. Read and stored only in this browser (stored only if you turn on “Remember”).
    Your search words, any acronym meaning you pick, the field you choose, and filtersOpenAlex (api.openalex.org), plus Crossref (api.crossref.org) and Europe PMC (ebi.ac.uk) if those sources are on.

    Titles you paste into “Compare with Google Scholar”Nowhere, unless you tap “Check OpenAlex”; then the missing titles go to OpenAlex.
    Saved papersNowhere. Kept in this browser.

    Code libraries are downloaded only when needed: pdf.js or mammoth.js from jsDelivr when you open a PDF or Word file, and the optional relevance model from jsDelivr and Hugging Face. These downloads do not include your data. Each service sees your IP address, as with any website.

    Cost and limits

    OpenAlex now meters its free API. Without a key you get about $0.10 of free use a day (roughly 30 searches here); with a free key, $1 a day (roughly 300). Paperscope shows what each search used. When the allowance runs out, OpenAlex refuses requests until midnight UTC; it does not bill you unless you have bought prepaid credit on openalex.org. Crossref and Europe PMC are free and need no key, but may slow down busy users.

    Settings

    Get one free at openalex.org/settings/api. Kept in this browser only.
